About

I am interested in how the human brain incrementally processes language , especially how different sources of linguistic information, such as syntax and semantics, are represented and whether and how they interact in the mind. To investigate these questions, my research combines behavioral experiments , EEG/ERP methods, and computational measures of language models , such as surprisal .

I am currently a Research Assistantat The Hong Kong Polytechnic University , supervised by Dr. Jiaqiang Zhu. I received my M.A. and B.A. from Sichuan University.

Research Experience

Master’s dissertation · Sichuan University

Coercion and predictability in Mandarin

I investigate coercion in Mandarin adverb–noun constructions, bringing together EEG, behavioral norms, and computational measures of predictability.

  • Designed the EEG experiment and stimuli, recruited 60 participants, and conducted EEG data collection.
  • Collected completion norms and semantic ratings, computed LLM surprisal, and modeled trial-level EEG responses using regression-based ERP estimation (rERP).

EEG · Completion norms · LLM surprisal · rERP

Collaborative project · October 2023–April 2025

Object size and semantic processing

This project examined cognitive and neural differences in processing words referring to small and large objects.

  • Recruited 228 participants across the behavioral and EEG experiments and organized subjective semantic ratings.
  • Preprocessed EEG data in MATLAB and contributed to multivariate pattern analysis using MVPA-Light.

Behavioral experiments · EEG preprocessing · MVPA

Collaborative project · September 2023–April 2025

Orthographic processing and the ND250

This project investigated the functional interpretation of the ND250 response by comparing handwritten and printed words.

  • Contributed to the 2 × 2 experimental design, prepared 300 stimuli, and independently conducted the EEG experiment after recruiting 30 participants.
  • Performed univariate ND250 analyses and exploratory mass-univariate analyses with false discovery rate correction.

Experimental design · EEG/ERP · Mass-univariate analysis

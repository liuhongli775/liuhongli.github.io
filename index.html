Research Assistant · The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Hongli Liu

Psycholinguistics · Cognitive Neuroscience · Computational Modeling

I study how linguistic knowledge is represented and processed in the human mind, with a particular interest in the relationship between categorical linguistic representations and continuous probabilistic information. I combine EEG/ERP experiments with computational models of language to investigate these questions.

Research themes

One research program, three connected perspectives.

01

Language comprehension & linguistic representation

Categorical and continuous representations; syntax–semantics interactions; prediction and integration during incremental comprehension.

02

Neural dynamics of language

EEG/ERP combined with regression-based ERP estimation, mass-univariate analysis, MVPA, and representational similarity analysis.

03

Computational psycholinguistics

Language-model surprisal, model-derived representations, human–LM comparison, and computational accounts of processing difficulty.

Selected research

Questions first. Methods in conversation.

My projects ask how categorical linguistic structure and continuous probabilistic information jointly shape behavioral and neural responses.

Current focus01

Mandarin Adverb–Noun Constructions

Coercion or Predictability?

Do apparently construction-specific neural effects reflect categorical grammatical processing or continuous expectation-related processes?

I combine EEG/ERP with predictors derived from pretrained language models, cloze probability, and semantic association to examine this question in Mandarin adverb–noun constructions.

  • EEG/ERP
  • GPT-2
  • LLaMA
  • Surprisal
  • rERP
  • LMM
  • Mass-univariate analysis
Poster

Handwritten and Printed Words

Orthographic Processing & the ND250

Does the 250-ms ERP difference reflect orthographic-lexicon activation or a prediction-error signal?

This project compares handwritten and printed words to investigate the functional interpretation of the ND250 during visual word recognition.

  • EEG/ERP
  • Experimental design
  • Univariate analysis
  • Mass-univariate analysis

Words Referring to Small and Large Objects

Object Size & Semantic Representation

Which conceptual dimensions organize the neural representation of words referring to differently sized objects?

This collaborative project combines behavioral experiments, EEG, multivariate decoding, and semantic ratings to study neural differences in word meaning.

  • Behavioral experiments

Selected computational projects

Computational work as part of the science.

These research workflows connect linguistic hypotheses, computational predictors, and neural data.

Research workflow

LLM Surprisal for Mandarin Sentence Processing

Research problem
Quantify graded contextual expectation in Mandarin sentence materials.
Data / model
Sentence stimuli, cloze norms, behavioral measures, and trial-level EEG.
Method
GPT-2 / LLaMA · Hugging Face · Python
Output
Word-level surprisal predictors aligned with human behavioral and neural data.

Analysis workflow

Regression-based ERP Analysis

Research problem
Estimate overlapping categorical and continuous effects without reducing trial-level variability to condition averages.
Data / model
Single-trial EEG with linguistic and computational predictors.
Method
rERP · Linear mixed models · R / Python
Output
Time-resolved estimates of predictor effects on neural activity.

Analysis workflow

Mass-univariate EEG Analysis

Research problem
Evaluate distributed effects across electrodes and time while controlling multiple comparisons.
Data / model
Epoched EEG and condition- or trial-level predictors.
Method
Mass-univariate testing · FDR correction · MATLAB
Output
Exploratory spatiotemporal inference for ERP effects.

Analysis workflow

MVPA / RSA Pipelines

Research problem
Relate distributed neural response patterns to conceptual and model-derived representational spaces.
Data / model
EEG patterns, semantic ratings, and representational models.
Method
MVPA-Light · Regression RSA · MATLAB
Output
Decoding and model-alignment analyses of neural representations.
